Game Overview

A fast-paced rhythmic runner where you guide Punky, a stubborn punk rock pug, through city obstacles to reach his band's show before the countdown timer hits zero.

Key Mechanics

  • Timer The core enemy is the clock. Every second left is points.
  • The Pug Sit Deplete your stamina and Punky sits. Complete a rapid "crinkle bag" rhythm mini-game to get him up.
  • Distractions Dropped lamb treats and squirrels lure Punky off the optimal path.
  • Mosh Pit Zoomies Collect 3 studded collars for a screen-clearing invincibility sprint.

Boss: Princess the Pop Poodle

A pristine show poodle on a tour bus firing synth-pop music notes and hairspray. Defeat her by splashing her with city grime from a fire hydrant.
